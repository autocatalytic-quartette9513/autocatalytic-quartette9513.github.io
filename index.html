📖 What Is ComfyClean?

ComfyClean is an add-on for Adobe Photoshop built for people who work with manhwa and manga pages. Cleaning a page the traditional way means cloning, healing, patching and painting over every speech bubble, every sound effect and every little watermark. It is slow, it is tedious, and it is very easy to leave a smudge behind.

ComfyClean replaces all of that with a single click. You select the text, the bubble, the SFX or the watermark you want gone, and the plugin sends that small area to a local ComfyUI installation. The AI model paints new artwork to fill the gap, matching the surrounding lines and shading. The result comes back into your Photoshop document as an edit you can undo at any time.

Everything happens on your own computer. No uploads, no accounts, no cloud credits, no waiting in a queue. Your pages never leave your machine, and your original artwork is never overwritten.

⚙️ How It Works in Three Steps

  1. Select. Use any Photoshop selection tool to draw around the text, bubble, SFX or watermark you want removed.
  2. Click. Press the ComfyClean button in the plugin panel. The selected area is sent to your local ComfyUI server.
  3. Review. The filled result comes back on its own layer. Zoom in, compare, and undo instantly if you prefer the original.

✨ What You Can Remove

  • Dialogue text inside speech bubbles, including tight, curved or overlapping letters.
  • Entire empty speech bubbles and thought balloons that you want painted away.
  • Sound effects and stylised SFX lettering drawn straight onto the artwork.
  • Watermarks, credit lines, scan marks and stray page numbers.
  • Small objects, dust, scratches and compression artefacts.
  • Localised touch-ups where a previous clean left a visible patch.

🖌️ Your First Clean, Step by Step

  1. Open Photoshop and load the manhwa page you want to work on.
  2. Make sure your local ComfyUI is running in the background.
  3. Open the ComfyClean panel from the plugins menu so the controls are visible.
  4. Choose the selection tool you are comfortable with and draw carefully around one piece of text or one bubble. A little margin around the letters helps the AI blend the fill into the artwork.
  5. Press the clean button and wait a few seconds while the local model works.
  6. Check the result on its own layer. If something looks off, undo and try a tighter or looser selection.
  7. Repeat across the page, then export your finished chapter as usual.

💡 Tips for Cleaner Results

  • Clean one bubble at a time rather than the whole page in a single selection. Smaller areas give the model more context to work with.
  • Leave a thin border of original artwork inside your selection so the fill knows which lines and tones to continue.
  • Work at full resolution. Downscaled pages lose the fine line work the model relies on.
  • Try a different Flux model when a fill looks soft. Klein, Kontext and Fill each handle line art a little differently.
  • Keep your edits on separate layers so you can compare the cleaned version against the original at any time.

❓ Common Questions

Does ComfyClean send my pages anywhere?

No. The plugin only talks to a ComfyUI server running on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded, and no internet connection is needed once everything is installed.

Will it overwrite my original file?

No. ComfyClean is non-destructive. Results arrive as new layers and your source artwork stays untouched.

Do I need to pay for AI credits?

No. All the AI work happens locally, so there are no subscriptions and no per-image costs.

What if the fill looks wrong?

Undo, adjust your selection, and run it again. Slightly different selections often produce noticeably better blends.

Can I use it on manga as well as manhwa?

Yes. Any page with text, bubbles, SFX or watermarks on artwork can be cleaned the same way.

🔒 Privacy at a Glance

ComfyClean is built around a local-only workflow. Your selections, your pages and your finished files stay on your own computer. There is no telemetry, no cloud account and no external processing. You control the models, you control the machine, and you control the artwork.
